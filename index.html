HOLC redlining, 1935–1940

Reading the Redlining Record

The Home Owners' Loan Corporation's maps are well known. The typed forms behind them get less attention. These studies read those forms closely: the words the appraisers used, and whether their grades followed the numbers they recorded.

Sources

All boundaries, grades, map scans and form transcriptions come from Mapping Inequality: Redlining in New Deal America, by Robert K. Nelson, LaDale Winling, Richard Marciano, Nathan Connolly, et al., in American Panorama, ed. Robert K. Nelson and Edward L. Ayers (Digital Scholarship Lab, University of Richmond). Form transcriptions are from their area description data.

A note on language

The forms are quoted as transcribed, including the racial and ethnic terms the appraisers used. They are shown because that language is the evidence: it is how a federal agency wrote race into its definition of risk.
