HOLC Residential Security Map · April 3, 1937

Newport News, as the HOLC graded it

On April 3, 1937, the federal Home Owners' Loan Corporation finished its "Residential Security Map" of Newport News, splitting the city into 17 areas and grading each by mortgage risk. No neighborhood received an A. The original sheet sits underneath; slide to fade between it and the digitized areas, and tap an area for its grade and size.
